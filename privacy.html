Deuce: Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Deuce: 1v1 Tennis does not collect, store or share personal data. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it sends nothing to its developer.

What stays on the phone

Match progress, records and settings are saved on the phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

Online matches and the Survival leaderboard use Apple's Game Center. Apple runs the sign-in, matchmaking and leaderboard under Apple's privacy policy. During an online match the two phones exchange the moves of the match and each player's Game Center nickname, and the game keeps only the player's own win and loss count.

Video clips

Highlight clips are made on the phone. A clip leaves the phone only when the player shares or saves it through the iOS share sheet.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children.
