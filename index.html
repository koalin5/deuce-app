For help, bug reports or ideas, email esteban@42cal.com. A note on the iPhone model and what happened helps.

Online matches

Online play needs a Game Center account. Sign in under Settings, Game Center on the iPhone, then open Online in the game. Both players need the latest version of Deuce.

Learning the shots

Quick Game has eight short drills under the Play button that teach each shot.
